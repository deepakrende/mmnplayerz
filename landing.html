Questions

The short answers.

Does MMN include channels?

No. MMN is only a player. You load a playlist or account from a provider you are licensed to use, and MMN plays it.

Which devices work?

Any device with a modern browser: iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux, and smart TVs with a built-in browser.

Why won't a channel play?

The channel may be offline or blocked in your region, or your provider may allow only one connection at a time. Close other players using the same account, and try switching between HLS and MPEG-TS when you connect.

Are my logins stored?

Not on our server. Streams pass through it to reach your browser, but links and credentials aren't logged. If you tick "Remember on this device", they are saved in your own browser only.

Is there an app?

Not yet. The website works on all devices today, and native apps for TVs and phones are planned.
